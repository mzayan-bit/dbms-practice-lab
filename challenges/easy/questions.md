# Challenge Suite: Easy — Unlabelled Practical SQL

> [!NOTE]
> In Challenge Mode, questions describe only the analytical goal.
> Determine the required SQL techniques independently. Write your queries in `attempts.sql`.

---

### Challenge E1: Department Contact Book
Produce a university directory listing every department's name, its building, and the full name and email of every instructor in that department. Departments without instructors should still appear in the list.

---

### Challenge E2: High Honor Students by Department
Which academic departments have an average student GPA greater than `3.40`? Display the department name, number of students, and their average GPA.

---

### Challenge E3: Unassigned Advising Workload
Find all students who have completed at least 20 credits but have not yet been assigned an academic advisor.

---

### Challenge E4: Popular Introductory Courses
Find all courses with 4 credits that have more than 1 section scheduled across any semester.
