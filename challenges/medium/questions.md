# Challenge Suite: Medium — Interview & Analytical SQL

> [!NOTE]
> In Challenge Mode, questions describe only the analytical goal.
> Determine the required SQL techniques independently. Write your queries in `attempts.sql`.

---

### Challenge M1: Faculty Teaching Load & Salary Efficiency
Calculate each department's total faculty payroll, total number of sections taught by faculty in that department, and the average cost per section taught (`total_payroll / total_sections`).

---

### Challenge M2: Outstanding Performers Above Departmental Mean
Find all students whose individual GPA is higher than the average GPA of their respective department, but who are NOT in the Computer Science department.

---

### Challenge M3: Course Prerequisite Depth
List every course title and the title of its direct prerequisite course. If the prerequisite course itself requires a prerequisite, show the grandparent prerequisite title as well in a third column (`prerequisite_of_prerequisite`).

---

### Challenge M4: Top Student in Every Department
Identify the single student with the highest GPA in each department. In case of ties, include all tied students.
